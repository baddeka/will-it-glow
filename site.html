What is limiting the measurement now

    Model. Cytoplasm and medium are treated as separate compartments exchanging H2O2 at . The cytoplasm is quasi-steady on a millisecond timescale; the medium is integrated explicitly because at low cell density it equilibrates over minutes. Anchors: endogenous production and resting cytoplasmic (Seaver & Imlay 2001, which together force ); OxyR half-activation at cytoplasmic H2O2 (Aslund 1999; Imlay reviews); oxidase , (Bakke 2007). The model reproduces the independently measured medium-to-cytoplasm gradient of without fitting.

    Read it as a design tool, not a prediction. Three quantities are unmeasured and any of them can close the branch: whether DAS crosses the inner membrane at all, how selective the oxidase is against the more abundant urinary acetylpolyamines, and whether creatinine can be measured in parallel.
